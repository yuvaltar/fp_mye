## Inputs, for stock *i* at position *p*

- `errors[q] = log(realized_vol[q] / pred_k[q])` for the four calibrated skills. The window is rows *p* − 5 − L + 1 to *p* − 5: an outcome is known 5 trading days after its prediction, the current supervisor's rule.
- Spread: `log pred_k[p] − mean_k log pred_k[p]`; earnings flag at *p*.
- Map at *p*: `correlation_adjacency_series` on the frozen panel (window 120, k = 5, market-adjusted), the sector map, or the correlation map with stocks permuted by `itransformer._shuffled` (the same control as Step 9). `nograph` has no graph layer.
- Rows without a full window are not used; every scored row (2019 on) has one.

## Network and training

GRU (16 units) over the window → concatenated with spread and flag → linear to 16 with tanh → 0, 1 or 2 graph layers `H + g·A·H·W` (`g` starts at 0, learning rate 0.02 as in `itransformer.py`) → linear head to 4 logits, initialised at zero → softmax → `sum_k w_k pred_k`. Float64. Loss: mean QLIKE of the combination.

Walk-forward: refit every 63 trading days, on every row whose outcome is known (position ≤ *r* − 5), then predict the next 63 days. Two schedules: from 2019-01-02 to the end of 2019 (selection) and from 2020-01-02 (test). Each fit: Adam, learning rate 1e-3, 300 steps of 32 dates (all stocks of a date together), fixed before any result. Seeds fixed, runs one after another. Confidence: `exp(−mean QLIKE)` of the model's own last 120 known predictions (0.5 before any), as `Supervisor`.

## Order of choices (all on 2019)

1. `nograph` with L = 10, 20, 60, three seeds each; keep the lowest mean 2019 QLIKE. If it is not below the equal-weight 2019 QLIKE, stop and write the negative result.
2. `corr` with 1 and 2 graph layers at that L; keep the better. `sector` and `shuffled` use the same L and layer count.
3. The candidate is the variant with the lowest mean 2019 QLIKE among the four.
4. Test: the four variants, three seeds, once. **Go** if the candidate's mean test QLIKE is below the supervisor, the equal-weight average and the best single skill, and every seed is below equal weight. A gap to the strongest of those three smaller than the seed spread is reported as "not settled".

## Gates (before any result counts; checked by the runner and by tests)

1. `apply_supervisor` with `Supervisor(window=120, temperature=0.25)` on the saved calibrated predictions reproduces `supervisor` to 2e-6 (the plan said 1e-9, impossible: `predictions.csv` is saved with 6 decimals, so the rounding alone gives about 1e-6).
2. The untrained network (head at zero) reproduces `equal_weight` to the same 2e-6 (the exact 1/4 weights are tested to 1e-12 on synthetic data).
3. Leakage: corrupting outcomes revealed after *p*, predictions after *p* and prices after *p* leaves the inputs and the walk-forward weights up to *p* unchanged.
4. The frozen panel's date at each `position` equals `date` in `predictions.csv`.
